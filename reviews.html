Reviews

"Buckingham Palace is not only the residence of the King and Queen of England but also the place where they and their staff work. The rooms are decorated so beautifully that it feels like you are in a dreamland. Each room has its own function and purpose according to the needs and running of the kingdom." - Anonymous

"Big Ben is a London icon that always manages to captivate the eye. This majestic clock tower stands proudly on the banks of the River Thames, with its distinctive chime and classic, elegant vibe. At night, its illumination adds to the dramatic atmosphere. It's simple yet has a charm that will make you want to return!" - Anonymous

"The place is located in the center of London. From the London Eye, we can see all corners of London from a height. The ticket price is 40 pounds for adults and 30 pounds for children. The location of the London Eye is opposite the British Parliament building or Big Ben Tower. Around the location, there are also a number of game rides." - Another Anonymous User

"Tower Bridge, is a bridge stretching over the River Thames in London, Great Britain. This bridge combines two bridge designs that lift and hang. Located adjacent to the London Tower makes this bridge was named Tower Bridge. This bridge was completed in 1894, and this is one of the icons of London. The height of the bridge is about 45 meters from the sea level. The traveler can walk on Tower Bridge and pass through a bridge made of thick transparent glass so that you can see the traffic below it." - Yet Another Anonymous User

"I've visited this iconic cathedral several times, once on a regular tour and several times for Mass. Although I once paid an entrance fee to climb the dome, attending a service allowed me to hear their magnificent choir for free. The architecture is stunning, and the spiritual atmosphere during the choir's performances is truly moving. The free entry doesn't allow access to the upper gallery, but the views of the interior and altar are still well worth seeing. This is a must-see in London for its history; be sure to check the schedule if you want to enjoy the music." - Our Final Anonymous User
